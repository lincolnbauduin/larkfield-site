Cobalt Health

Traded cheap leads for qualified ones

Cost per lead was on the dashboard and it was the wrong number. We ran LinkedIn against paid search for a month and reported both, including the part where the blended cost went up.

Stopped them buying their own brand

Nearly a third of spend went on branded search they already won organically. Moving it into unowned categories cost more per lead and produced more pipeline.
