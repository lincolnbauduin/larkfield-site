Working together

How many clients do you take?

Six. That is the whole book, and it is deliberate — it is the number two senior people can run without the reporting slipping. A seventh only happens when a slot opens.

Who actually works on the account?

The people you meet on the call. There is no junior handover after signing, because there is no bench to hand it to.

What do the first two months look like?

Two weeks auditing before we spend anything, then a written plan of what we would stop and start. Most accounts get smaller before they get bigger.

Will you tell us if we are not a fit?

Yes, on the first call, and we will usually name who to talk to instead. A bad fit costs us more than the fee is worth.

Fees & spend

What does a retainer cost?

Retainers start at $4,500 a month for a single channel, and most clients are on the Demand tier at $8,500. Full breakdown on the services page.

Do you take a percentage of ad spend?

No. The fee is fixed, so we have no financial reason to want your budget larger than it should be. Every agency says this; ours is the only kind of agreement where it is structurally true.

Is there a minimum term?

Three months on Growth and Demand, six on Embedded. Paid acquisition takes a quarter to tell you anything reliable, and we would rather not take money for a month that cannot answer the question.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do, always. We work inside your accounts, and if we part ways you keep the history rather than starting from zero.

Reporting

When do reports arrive?

By the second working day of the month, every month. Not "early the following week".

What is in them?

Spend, leads and cost per lead by channel against the previous month, what changed and why, and what we are doing next. Every figure names where it came from.

If a month was bad it is the first line, not the last page.

Do we get told about problems mid-month?

Yes. Anything pacing over 80% of budget before the 15th gets flagged the day it happens, along with what we propose doing about it.
