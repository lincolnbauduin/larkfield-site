How we work

The same four steps on every account, so you always know what week it is.

  1. Audit before we spend

    Two weeks reading your account, your CRM and your last four months of reporting. We tell you what we would stop before we tell you what we would start.

  2. Agree the number that matters

    One primary metric per account, written down. Cost per qualified lead, pipeline created, demo requests — whichever one your board actually asks about.

  3. Run it, and pace it

    Weekly pacing against budget, not monthly surprises. Anything over 80% before the 15th gets flagged to you the day it happens.

  4. Report on the same day, always

    Reports land by the second working day. Every figure cites where it came from, and a bad month is the first line rather than the last page.
